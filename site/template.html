Tailwater

How much irrigation water does it cost to protect the worst seasons, not just the average one? A learned controller with a constraint on its worst 5% of yields, tested on 74 real Florida springs.

Spring maize, 1 March – 28 June Daily decisions: 0 / 5 / 10 / 20 mm Yield: FAO-33 stage model
One season, four fields

Same weather, different controllers

Pick a year from the Belle Glade record and watch four fields get the same rain and heat. Each controller was tuned or trained on other years only, so this season is new to all of them.

Water against the worst years
